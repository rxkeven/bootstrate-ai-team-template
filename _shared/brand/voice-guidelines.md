## Default voice (until customized)

Warm. Calm. Confident. Simple. Use this voice for all external-facing copy unless a specific brand brief overrides it.

## Default tone by context

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Client email | Warm, direct, specific. Lead with the answer or next step. |
| Marketing copy | Confident, plainspoken, focused on the reader's problem. |
| Product UI microcopy | Brief, clear, helpful. No clever wordplay. |
| Apology or recovery | Direct, accountable, no hedging. State what went wrong and what happens next. |
| Sales follow-up | Friendly, specific, low-pressure. |

## Default voice principles

- Active voice. The subject does the verb.
- Direct sentences. One idea per sentence where possible.
- Short paragraphs. Two to four sentences typical.
- No hedging. State the recommendation; reasoning can follow.
- Concrete over abstract. Name the thing. Use numbers where they exist.
- Plain language. If a 12-year-old wouldn't understand the sentence, rewrite it.

## Default words to favor

- "you" and "your" over "the customer" / "users"
- "we" over the company name in conversational copy
- Verbs over noun phrases ("decide" not "make a decision")
- Specific time references ("Friday at 3pm") over vague ones ("soon")
